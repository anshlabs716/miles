# Privacy Policy

MILES is built to work without accounts, without ads, and without mandatory
analytics.

## What MILES does NOT do

- No ads and no ad tracking
- No mandatory account — not a MILES account, not a Google account
- No mandatory Google Play Services
- No mandatory analytics or telemetry
- No selling or sharing of your activity data

## What stays on your device

Your activity history, saved routes, goals, fitness pet and settings are stored
in a local database on your device. This data is not uploaded anywhere by the app.

## What leaves your device, and only when you ask

- **Maps** — map tiles and routing requests are sent to the map/routing service
  you are using (OpenStreetMap tiles, OSRM routing) so the map can load. These
  requests include the map area you are viewing.
- **Weather** — weather lookups send your approximate location to the weather
  service so it can return local conditions.
- **Your own exports** — when you export or share a file, that file goes
  wherever you send it.

## Permissions

Permissions are requested only when the feature that needs them is used, and the
app keeps working without them wherever possible:

- Location — for maps, GPS tracking and navigation
- Activity recognition — for step counting
- Notifications — for workout and reminder alerts
- Bluetooth — for heart-rate straps and cadence sensors

## Your data, your control

You can export your data (GPX, CSV, JSON or a full MILES backup), back it up, and
delete it — including deleting your history — at any time from inside the app.

## Contact

Open an issue on the project's source repository for privacy questions.
