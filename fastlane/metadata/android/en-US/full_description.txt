MILES is a privacy-focused, local-first activity tracker for Android.

Track your real activity with step counting, GPS routes, workouts, Bluetooth sensors and Health Connect. View your activity history and statistics while keeping your data under your control.

Features:
• Real step and activity tracking
• GPS route recording
• Walking, running, cycling and hiking workouts
• OpenStreetMap-based mapping
• Bluetooth Low Energy sensor support
• Health Connect integration
• JSON, CSV, GPX and TCX data support
• Movement reminders
• Custom themes and launcher icons
• Accessibility options
• Local-first design with no MILES account or subscription

MILES does not intentionally generate fake GPS, heart-rate or step data.

Wear OS support is developed as a separate companion project.
