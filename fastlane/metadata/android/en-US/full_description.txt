MILES is a privacy-first activity and step tracker for Android. Every number it shows comes from real sensors — the pedometer, GPS and your own heart rate — or it isn't shown at all.

Features:
• Real step counting and GPS activity tracking for walking, running, cycling, hiking and more
• Turn-by-turn navigation with voice guidance
• Saved routes and a route builder
• Progressive training plans
• Real heart rate, cadence and barometer support, plus Bluetooth heart-rate straps
• Daily calories, distance and active minutes, derived from your real measured activity
• Resting heart rate and personal records
• Fitness pet, weekly goals and badges
• Offline-friendly local database, export and backup in standard formats (GPX, CSV, JSON)

Privacy:
• No ads
• No account required — not a MILES one, not a Google one
• No mandatory analytics
• Your activity data stays on your device and can be exported or deleted at any time

No Google Play Services required. No proprietary map SDK: maps use OpenStreetMap data.
